Privacy Policy — Pixel Hoops

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pixel Hoops (the “game”) is a basketball game for iOS. This policy explains what data the game processes and how.

Data controller

The game’s developer (“we”). Privacy contact: edgarakinis@gmail.com.

Game progress — stored on your device

Your best score, total points, coins, purchased items (sneakers, jerseys, balls, locations) and settings are stored only on your device in the standard iOS preferences store. We do not receive this data and do not store it on our servers.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your best score and total points to Apple’s Game Center leaderboards together with your Game Center nickname. Other players can see your nickname and scores in the leaderboards. This is processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can stop it at any time by signing out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases (Apple)

Coin packs are sold through the App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The game shows optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad to continue a game or to get free coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language and app information;
  • device identifiers, such as the identifier for vendors (the game does not ask for tracking permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to Google);
  • ad interactions, such as views and taps.

Google uses this data to show, measure and protect ads, as described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options.

We only receive aggregated reports from AdMob (for example, number of ads shown and earnings), not data about individual players.

What the game does NOT do

  • No accounts and no collection of names, emails or contacts.
  • No analytics of our own.
  • No selling of personal data.

Storage and deletion

Game progress stays on the device while the game is installed; deleting the game deletes it. Game Center data is managed by Apple, and advertising data by Google, under their own policies. You can reset your device identifiers in iOS Settings.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Consent for ads can be changed in the game (Settings → Privacy options). Because we do not keep your personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google. For any questions, contact edgarakinis@gmail.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and the text on this page.
