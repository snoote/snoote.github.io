Hoop Kicks — Support

A pixel-art basketball game for iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email edgarakinis@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version — we usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I play?

Swipe the ball toward the hoop. Every basket gives you points, and every point is a coin. Clean swishes and rare events give bonus coins.

What do sneakers, balls and courts do?

Spend coins in the Shop. Each sneaker pair has a small perk (for example a wider hoop or extra second chances), each ball has its own power, and courts change where you play. Equip items in the Shop.

I bought coins but didn’t get them.

Make sure you have an internet connection and reopen the game — pending App Store purchases are delivered on the next launch. If coins still don’t arrive, email us with the date of purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress disappeared.

Progress (coins, items, records, login streak) is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, also in your iCloud. After reinstalling, open the game with the same Apple ID and wait a few seconds — your progress comes back. It also syncs between your iPhone and iPad. Without iCloud, deleting the game deletes the progress; your best score and total points still remain in the Game Center leaderboards.

I don’t see myself in the Top 10.

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center. Scores are sent to the leaderboard after each game.

Why does an ad play after a game?

Ads keep the game free. A short ad may play when a game ends — at most about once a minute, and not after a game you continued by watching an ad.

The free coins / “watch an ad” button is not available.

Free coins can be claimed once every 24 hours, and ads need an internet connection. Sometimes no ad is available for a short while — try again later.

How do I change ad privacy settings?

Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): in the game, open Settings → Privacy options. Tracking permission: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Can I play left-handed?

Yes — open Settings (gear icon on the main screen) and switch the shooting hand.
